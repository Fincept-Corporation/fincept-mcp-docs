11 tools in 3 modules, plus `scan_catalog`, which lists them from inside your agent. All are free; requests to the market data source are paced server-wide, and a paced request answers `busy` with the seconds to wait.

| Module | Toolset | Covers | Tools |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Market screener](/reference/scanner/screen) | `scan_screen` | Live screens of any market (stocks in 68 countries, ETFs, crypto pairs and coins, forex, futures, CFDs, bonds, options, economic series) on any of about 4,000 fields: price and volume on any timeframe, performance, technical indicators and ratings, candlestick patterns, fundamentals, dividends, earnings dates and analyst targets; conditions, and/or groups, crosses and 73 index universes; field search, markets, operators and 44 ready-made screens. | 4 |
| [Price history](/reference/scanner/bars) | `scan_bars` | Bars for any instrument on any interval (count or date range, split- or dividend-adjusted), screens of many instruments' bars with custom formulas (crossovers, breakouts, streaks, pullbacks, squeezes), and many instruments' returns over any calendar month, quarter, year or date range. | 3 |
| [Company fundamentals](/reference/scanner/fundamentals) | `scan_fundamentals` | Income statement, balance sheet, cash flow and statistics by year, half, quarter or TTM; earnings against estimates; dividend history; revenue by business and by region. | 4 |
