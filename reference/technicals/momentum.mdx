Toolset `ta_momentum`: 3 tools.

| Tool | What it does | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ta_momentum` | A momentum or trend-strength indicator on OHLCV bars (or one series for the close-only ones such as RSI, CMO, MOM, ROC, TRIX, TSI): its output(s) aligned to the dates with the warm-up reported, the last values and a line chart. |  |
| `ta_oscillator` | A moving-average oscillator on the closes (or source, or one series; AO and AC read high/low): its lines aligned to the dates with the warm-up reported, a line chart and a bar chart of the histogram. |  |
| `ta_stochastic` | A stochastic oscillator from high/low/close bars (STOCHRSI from the closes or one series): %K and %D (KDJ also J) aligned to the dates, 0-100 scale, with the warm-up reported and a line chart. |  |

Inputs, limits and outputs are described in [Fincept Technicals](/guides/fincept-technicals). Full schemas: `fincept_describe_tool`.
