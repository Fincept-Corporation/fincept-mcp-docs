17 tools in 10 modules, plus `ta_catalog`, which lists them from inside your agent. All are free.

| Module | Toolset | Covers | Tools |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Cycle indicators (Hilbert transform)](/reference/technicals/cycle) | `ta_cycle` | Hilbert-transform dominant cycle period and phase, in-phase and quadrature components, sine and lead-sine waves, and the trend-versus-cycle mode. | 1 |
| [Any indicator](/reference/technicals/indicators) | `ta_indicators` | Every indicator by name: many at once over the same bars (one aligned table and chart), and each indicator's documentation: inputs, parameters with defaults and meaning, outputs, warm-up and unstable period, moving-average types. | 2 |
| [Math transforms and operators](/reference/technicals/math) | `ta_math` | Element-wise arccosine, arcsine, arctangent, ceiling, cosine, hyperbolic cosine, exponential, floor, natural and base-10 logarithm, sine, hyperbolic sine, square root, tangent and hyperbolic tangent; addition, subtraction, multiplication and division of two series; cumulative sum; rolling sum, highest, lowest, both, and the bar positions of the highest and lowest. | 2 |
| [Momentum indicators](/reference/technicals/momentum) | `ta_momentum` | RSI, ADX/ADXR/DX with the directional indicators and movement, Aroon, CCI, CMO, MFI, momentum and rate of change, TRIX, Williams %R, ultimate oscillator, balance of power, Coppock, DPO, efficiency ratio, Elder ray, forecast oscillator, Williams fractals, IMI, Qstick, TSI, VHF, vortex, Williams A/D; MACD (standard, any MA type, fixed 12/26), APO, PPO, awesome and accelerator oscillators; stochastic (slow, fast, of RSI), KDJ and the stochastic momentum index. | 3 |
| [Overlap studies](/reference/technicals/overlap) | `ta_overlap` | Indicators on the price's own scale: simple, exponential, weighted, double and triple exponential, triangular, Kaufman adaptive, MESA adaptive, T3, Hull, zero-lag, Wilder and volume-weighted moving averages, variable-period MA, Hilbert trendline, midpoint and midprice; Bollinger, Keltner, Donchian and acceleration bands; parabolic SAR (standard and extended) and SuperTrend. | 3 |
| [Candlestick patterns](/reference/technicals/patterns) | `ta_patterns` | All 61 Japanese candlestick patterns (doji family, hammer and hanging man, engulfing, harami, stars, three white soldiers and black crows, kicking, marubozu, hikkake, ...) on OHLC bars: one pattern's signal at every bar, or a scan of many patterns listing which fired on which dates. | 2 |
| [Price transforms](/reference/technicals/price-transform) | `ta_price_transform` | Average price, median price, typical price, weighted close, Heikin-Ashi candles and the average (mean absolute) deviation. | 1 |
| [Rolling statistics](/reference/technicals/statistics) | `ta_statistics` | Rolling beta and Pearson correlation of two series, rolling linear regression (end value, slope, intercept, angle, one-bar forecast), standard deviation, variance, nearest-rank percentile and percent rank. | 1 |
| [Volatility indicators](/reference/technicals/volatility) | `ta_volatility` | True range, average true range (ATR) and normalized ATR, average day range, Chaikin's volatility, the mass index and the relative volatility index. | 1 |
| [Volume indicators](/reference/technicals/volume) | `ta_volume` | On-balance volume, Chaikin accumulation/distribution line and oscillator, Chaikin money flow, Elder's force index, market facilitation index, negative and positive volume indexes, percentage volume oscillator, price-volume trend, relative volume and the cumulative VWAP. | 1 |
