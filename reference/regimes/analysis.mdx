Toolset `regime_analysis`: 2 tools.

| Tool | What it does | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `regime_statistics` | Statistics of a returns series under a regime path: per regime the number of periods, share of time, mean, volatility, annualised mean and volatility and their ratio, extremes and spell counts and lengths; every spell with its dates and compounded return; the empirical transition counts and probabilities; and the current spell. |  |
| `regime_markov_chain` | Analytics of a regime transition matrix on its own (e.g. data.model.transmat of a fit, or an assumed one): stationary distribution, persistence, expected duration and half-life of each regime, the eigenvalues and the half-life of convergence to the long run, the h-step regime forecast from the current probabilities (series + chart) and optionally a simulated regime path with its spells. |  |

Inputs, limits and outputs are described in [Fincept Regimes](/guides/fincept-regimes). Full schemas: `fincept_describe_tool`.
