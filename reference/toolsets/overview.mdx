440 platform tools in 18 toolsets, plus the engines [Fincept Stats](/reference/stats/overview), [Fincept Forecast](/reference/forecast/overview), [Fincept Allocation](/reference/allocation/overview), [Fincept Portfolio Optimizer](/reference/portfolio-optimizer/overview) and [Fincept Quant](/reference/quant/overview). Each tool belongs to one toolset; a toolset name is what `?toolsets=` and `fincept_search_tools` take.

| Toolset | Covers | Tools |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`market`](/reference/toolsets/market) | Quotes, candles, fundamentals, profiles, peers, option chains, futures curves, corporate actions, holidays and the economic calendar. | 13 |
| [`backtest`](/reference/toolsets/backtest) | Strategy backtests and parameter sweeps, templates, validation, cost profiles, saved strategies and run history. | 26 |
| [`algo`](/reference/toolsets/algo) | Algorithm library, versions, baskets and paper deployments with their metrics and trades. | 21 |
| [`crypto`](/reference/toolsets/crypto) | Crypto venues, markets, derivatives data, your crypto accounts and orders, risk, compliance, custody and DeFi. Read-only. | 76 |
| [`data`](/reference/toolsets/data) | Global economic and financial series from every provider, datasets, vendor fundamentals, adjusted prices, options data and scheduled refreshes. | 43 |
| [`filings`](/reference/toolsets/filings) | SEC EDGAR companies and filings, insiders, 13F holders, funds and SEDAR+. | 32 |
| [`deals`](/reference/toolsets/deals) | M&A deals, comparable-company builds, private placements, private raises and private credit. | 19 |
| [`portfolio`](/reference/toolsets/portfolio) | Portfolios, valuation, performance, attribution, risk, scenarios, ledger, tax lots, limits, marks and benchmarks. | 39 |
| [`paper`](/reference/toolsets/paper) | The Fincept Paper account: books, orders, positions, trades and equity curves. No real money. | 21 |
| [`workspace`](/reference/toolsets/workspace) | Watchlists, alerts, notes, notebooks, reports, chat sessions, custom indices, news monitors and scan watches. | 35 |
| [`research`](/reference/toolsets/research) | Ask Fincept's research model and list the available models. | 2 |
| [`agents`](/reference/toolsets/agents) | Server-side agent runs, schedules and memories: inspect, cancel, pause and delete. | 9 |
| [`account`](/reference/toolsets/account) | Credit balance, usage, the credit ledger and account notices. | 4 |
| [`analytics`](/reference/toolsets/analytics) | Portfolio analytics, attribution, technicals, macro factors, market regimes, option strategies, volatility surfaces and rule backtests. | 18 |
| [`screener`](/reference/toolsets/screener) | Stock screens and their past runs. | 3 |
| [`quantlab`](/reference/toolsets/quantlab) | Research experiments, trials, deflated Sharpe ratios, signals, correlation checks and lineage. | 13 |
| [`news`](/reference/toolsets/news) | News feed and search, sentiment, AI analysis and summaries, keyword monitors and automations. | 22 |
| [`altdata`](/reference/toolsets/altdata) | Geopolitical events, sovereign bonds and yield curves, maritime and AIS data, shipping flows, ports and trade statistics. | 44 |
