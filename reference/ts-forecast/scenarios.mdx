| Tool | What it does | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `tsforecast_simulate` | Simulates n_paths future paths of h periods from statistical models fitted to one series (y) or every series of a panel (data), with normal, Student t, Laplace, skew-normal, generalized-error or bootstrapped errors. |  |
