Toolset `changepoint_synthetic`: 1 tool.

| Tool | What it does | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `changepoint_simulate` | Draws a synthetic piecewise signal with known change points from the caller's seed (a teaching and benchmarking tool, never market data): piecewise-constant means, Gaussian correlation switches, linear-model coefficient changes or sine frequency changes. |  |

Inputs, limits and outputs are described in [Fincept Change Points](/guides/fincept-change-points). Full schemas: `fincept_describe_tool`.
